## 0. TL;DR

OpsRadar is building a structured partner program for **fractional COOs serving 3–5 marketing agencies each**. The core thesis: one fractional COO can convert 4 agency clients in ~60 days, returning **~$72K ARR per active partner** at a fully-loaded CAC under $250 — an order of magnitude better than every other channel we've tested.

**The bet:** 25 active partners in 90 days → 60–100 paid agencies → ~$90K MRR ceiling from this channel alone within 6 months.

**The mandatory unlock:** A working multi-client dashboard ("one login, all clients") plus a 25% recurring commission for the life of the customer.

## 1. Program Thesis

### 1.1 Why fractional COOs are OpsRadar's highest-ROI channel

Three structural reasons.

**(a) Pre-aggregated demand.** A fractional COO doesn't have one buying signal — they have 3–5. Every fractional COO is a portfolio of agencies that already pay $8–15K/month for operational competence. The buying objection ("do I need ops infrastructure?") is already resolved before we show up.

**(b) Trusted-advisor handoff.** A fractional COO recommending OpsRadar is not "an affiliate" to the agency owner — they're the person the owner already hired to make these decisions. Conversion rates on warm fractional-COO intros run 3–5x cold demos in comparable B2B SaaS (HubSpot Solutions Partner Program internal data; verified via PartnerStack benchmarks). **Hardened May 2026 data:** Optifai's 939-company benchmark study confirms warm intros / referrals convert at **15–25%** vs **1.5–2% for cold lists** — a **10–15x** gap, not 3–5x. The conservative model in §1.2 is conservative on purpose; the actual lift available from a trusted fractional COO intro is closer to the upper bound. ([Optifai 2026](https://optif.ai/learn/questions/cold-call-to-meeting-conversion-rate/))

**(c) Capacity unlock = product alignment.** Fractional COOs are capacity-bound — their bottleneck is the 2–4 weeks of manual audit work per new client. OpsRadar cuts that to hours. So we don't compete with their economics, we *expand* them: they can take a 5th or 6th client because we did the audit. Their incentive to deploy us aggressively is structural, not financial.

### 1.2 The unit economics math

| Variable | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Avg agency clients per active fractional COO | 4 | Section 10.3, path-a-strategy-2026.md |
| Avg OpsRadar ARPU per agency client | $1,500/mo | Growth tier (middle SKU); product-marketing.md |
| Months of expected client retention | 12+ | Vertical SaaS NRR benchmark 108–120% |
| **ARR per active partner (year 1)** | **$72,000** | 4 × $1,500 × 12 |
| Partner commission paid (25% recurring) | $18,000 | See §3 |
| **Net ARR per active partner (year 1)** | **$54,000** | After commission |

Even at a conservative 50% partner activation rate (12 of 25 partners hit 2+ clients), the channel produces ~$650K ARR in year one with founder time being the only real CAC.

**Conclusion:** Fractional COO is the only channel that is (a) demonstrably scalable to 50+ customers, (b) sub-$300 CAC, (c) compatible with a 90-day MRR target. It's not a "nice to have." It's the primary GTM motion for months 2–6.

### 1.4 What kills the thesis

This program fails if any of the following are true:
- We can't ship the multi-client dashboard (§6).
- We pay less than 20% recurring (fractionals will route to a competitor that does).
- We don't make their impact legible to *their* client (the dashboard has to make the COO look good, not just us).
- We try to compete with them on advisory (positioning rule: OpsRadar is the *tool* the COO deploys, never the *advisor* that replaces them).

## 2. The Partner Value Proposition

What an OpsRadar Fractional COO Partner actually gets — in priority order, because we'll lead with #1 in recruitment.

### 2.1 Capacity multiplier (the lead value)
- Onboarding audit cut from 2–4 weeks → ~2 hours (OpsRadar auto-ingests their stack and produces the first health report).
- Quarterly review prep: zero — it's the dashboard.
- Reclaimed capacity = 1–2 additional billable clients per year. At $10K/mo per client, that's **$120K–$240K in additional fractional COO revenue** independent of OpsRadar commission.

### 2.2 Recurring commission (see §3 for full structure)
- **25% recurring for the life of the customer**, no vesting cliff.
- A partner with 4 clients on Growth ($1.5K) earns **$1,500/month recurring** ($18K/year/partner) on top of their advisory fees.

### 2.3 Client-facing impact dashboard
- Branded "Operations Report" the COO can share with the agency owner in their monthly/QBR meetings: utilization, margin, scope drift, recovered $.
- One-click export to PDF with the partner's logo.
- This is the single biggest reason a fractional COO will choose OpsRadar over building bespoke spreadsheets — it makes their advisory *visible and measurable* to their client.

### 2.4 Free OpsRadar Pro account for their own practice
- Every Verified Partner gets a perpetual free Pro seat ($3K/mo value) for their own fractional practice (one dashboard tracking *their portfolio* — utilization across clients, ARR managed, alerts).
- Doubles as their training environment and product expertise builder.

### 2.5 Co-marketing (Certified Partner tier and above)
- Joint webinar (1 per quarter, OpsRadar drives the list).
- Podcast guesting reciprocation: the partner gets booked on the OpsRadar podcast; OpsRadar founder appears on partners' clients' podcasts.
- Partner spotlight on opsradar.com/partners (do-follow link).
- Co-bylined blog post on "What a Fractional COO actually does" (drives both their pipeline and ours).

### 2.6 Joint roadmap input (Strategic Partner only)
- Quarterly 60-min product call with the founder. Partner sees what's on the roadmap and can flag the 1–2 things their clients keep asking for.
- Not a vote; a signal channel. Documented but non-binding.

### 2.7 White-label / co-branded — **DECISION: NO white-label. YES co-branded.**

**No white-label.** A partner cannot resell OpsRadar under their own brand. Rationale:
- Destroys our brand surface area in the exact ICP we're trying to own.
- Creates a reseller class with conflicting economics (they'll demand 40–60% margin, training, and tier-1 support — none of which scales).
- Splits the data flywheel: anonymized cross-customer benchmarks are our long-term moat (path-a §10.5). White-label resellers will demand we silo their customer data from the benchmark corpus, killing the moat.
- Solo founder cannot enforce reseller quality controls.

**Yes co-branded.** Inside the partner's client-facing reports and dashboards: the report header carries *both* logos ("OpsRadar — Operations brief prepared by [Partner Practice Name]"). Strong enough to make the partner look authored, weak enough that the agency owner knows OpsRadar is the tool. No public website/landing surface co-branding.

**Reseller pathway closed for year one.** Revisit in 2027 once the brand has 200+ logos and the team can support a channel ops role.

## 3. Commission Structure

### 3.1 The numbers

| Lever | OpsRadar Setting | Norm | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Recurring %** | **25%** | 20% (HubSpot, most SaaS) — 30% (aggressive challengers) | 25% beats the path-a-strategy floor (20%) and the HubSpot/PartnerStack default (20%). We're a startup competing with a 13K-member network's word-of-mouth — we need to be *visibly more generous* than the brand-name programs they're already used to. |
| **First-month bonus** | **$0** | $50–$300 common | We deliberately skip a bounty. Recurring-only filters for partners who believe in retention. Bounty-driven affiliates churn the program and the customer. |
| **Vesting** | **For the life of the customer** (no cliff) | 12 months (HubSpot Providers); 36 months (HubSpot Gold+); lifetime (rare premium programs) | Lifetime is the strongest moat against a competitor poaching our partners with a "we pay forever, they only pay 12 months" pitch. The cost is modest because (a) median SaaS customer LTV is 24–36 mo, (b) lifetime aligns the partner's economic incentive with retention, which is our biggest risk factor. |
| **Tier escalation** | **Yes — 25% → 30% at 5 clients, 35% at 10 clients** | Tiered structures common (PartnerStack, HubSpot point system) | Tier escalation only on *new* deals after qualifying for the tier. Existing recurring stays at the tier when the deal closed. Prevents retroactive payout shock. |
| **Expansion credit** | **Yes — partner gets % on all expansion ARR from their referred clients, forever** | Mixed; HubSpot does, many don't | Critical for OpsRadar specifically because our land-and-expand thesis (path-a §10.4) means a referred Starter at $500 should naturally upgrade to Growth or Pro. If the partner doesn't share in the upside, they have an incentive to bring us their *biggest* clients only. Paying on expansion makes them happy to bring us Starters too. |
| **Clawback** | **First 60 days only** | 30–90 days standard | If the agency cancels in the first 60 days, paid commission is clawed back from the next payout. After 60 days, no clawback even on churn — protects the partner's predictable income. |
| **Payout cadence** | **Monthly, 30 days in arrears** | Net 30–45, monthly or quarterly | Monthly is the partner-friendly choice; PartnerStack handles it via Stripe sync. |
| **Payment method** | Stripe Connect → ACH or international wire via Rewardful | — | See §7 tool decision. |

### 3.2 Worked example: a partner over 24 months

- Month 1: brings 1 agency, Growth tier ($1,500/mo). Partner earns $375/mo.
- Month 3: brings 2nd agency ($1,500/mo). Partner now earns $750/mo. Still Verified tier (1–2 clients).
- Month 5: brings 3rd agency. Promoted to Certified Partner. $1,125/mo.
- Month 9: brings 4th and 5th agency. Promoted to "Certified+" (5+ clients = 30% on new deals). Existing 4 clients stay at 25% = $1,500/mo. New 5th client at 30% of $1,500 = $450/mo. Total: **$1,950/mo.**
- Month 12: original 4 clients all upgrade Starter → Growth, or 2 of them expand to Pro. Partner share of expansion: another $500–$1,000/mo.
- **Year-1 partner take: ~$15K. Year-2 run-rate at retention: ~$25K.** Plus the capacity-unlock revenue from §2.1.

### 3.3 What we explicitly will not do
- No SPIFs/bonuses for "5 deals in 30 days" — encourages bad-fit deals.
- No commission on demo-only ("MQL bounty") — only on paid conversion.
- No retroactive rate cuts. If we ever lower the commission %, existing partners are grandfathered at their current rate for the life of every existing customer.
- No exclusivity. Partners can recommend competitors. (We win on product, not contract.)

## 4. Partner Tiers

Three tiers, gated by active paid clients (paying ≥ $500/mo for ≥ 60 days, churn-adjusted).

### Tier 1 — Verified Partner (1–2 active clients)

**Criteria to enter:**
- Signed partner agreement
- Completed 90-min OpsRadar Foundations certification (self-serve video + 10-question quiz)
- LinkedIn profile indicates fractional COO / fractional ops / agency consultancy

**What they get:**
- 25% recurring commission, lifetime
- Free Pro account for their own practice
- Multi-client dashboard (full functionality)
- Asset library access (slides, scripts, email templates — §8)
- Listed on opsradar.com/partners with logo, bio, contact link
- Slack channel access (#partners-general)

**What they don't get:**
- Co-marketing slots
- Founder calls
- Custom commission

### Tier 2 — Certified Partner (3–5 active clients)

**Criteria to enter:**
- 3+ active paid clients for ≥ 60 days
- Net retention ≥ 90% on referred clients in trailing 90 days
- 1 client video testimonial (60–90 sec, partner facilitates)

**What they get (additions):**
- 25% on existing, 30% on new deals from client #5 onward
- 1 guaranteed joint webinar / podcast / blog asset per quarter (OpsRadar produces, partner co-stars)
- "Certified" badge on directory listing (top of search results)
- Direct line to founder via Slack DM
- Quarterly 30-min business review with OpsRadar CSM (or founder while we're solo)

### Tier 3 — Strategic Partner (6+ active clients, or $10K+ MRR sourced)

**Criteria to enter:**
- 6+ active paid clients OR $10K+ sourced MRR
- Customer retention ≥ 95% trailing 12 months
- Public case study willingness (named, photographed, on opsradar.com)
- 2-year minimum commitment to the partner agreement (renewable)

**What they get (additions):**
- 35% on new deals from client #10 onward (still 25/30 on existing tiers)
- Custom commission structure available (e.g., flat $X per closed deal for prepaid annual)
- Quarterly roadmap call with founder
- On-stage slot at OpsRadar annual customer event (when it exists)
- Co-investment in mutual events ($2–5K marketing fund per year, matched 1:1)
- Joint customer-facing case study (OpsRadar markets it, partner uses it for their own sales)
- First look at new products / private beta access

### Demotion rules
- Reviewed quarterly. If active client count drops below the tier floor for 2 consecutive quarters, partner is demoted.
- Commission rates on existing customers do not drop on demotion — only the tier badge and benefit access.

## 5. Partner Onboarding — 90-Day Plan

### Pre-day 1: agreement & access (1 hour partner time)
- Sign partner agreement (DocuSign, plain-English 4-pager — not a 20-page MSA).
- Receive welcome email with:
  - Unique referral link (Rewardful)
  - Partner Slack invite
  - Asset library URL
  - Calendar link to book Foundations certification call (optional — they can self-serve)
  - Free Pro account provisioned
- Stripe Connect onboarding (5 min).

### Week 1: certification & first prospect
- Day 1: Foundations certification (90 min self-serve video + quiz). Covers: product walkthrough, 4 pillars (OPERATE/OBSERVE/BRAIN/GUARD), 5 most common objections, the discovery call script (§8), the COO-led onboarding flow.
- Day 2–3: Partner identifies their top 3 client candidates from their existing book of business. Picks 1 to lead with.
- Day 4–7: Partner sends "introducing OpsRadar" email to that client (template in §8). Books a joint demo with OpsRadar founder.

### Week 2–4: first client onboarded (white-glove)
- Joint demo: partner positions, founder runs product walkthrough, partner closes.
- Founder handles all friction: contract, payment, integration setup. Partner observes so they can solo the next one.
- Goal: first paid signup by **Day 21**. (If not by Day 30, founder schedules a 15-min "what's blocking" call.)

### Month 2: second client + first co-marketing asset
- Partner repeats the playbook solo on client #2. Founder available on Slack, not on the call.
- OpsRadar produces partner's spotlight page on opsradar.com/partners (60-min interview, content team writes).
- LinkedIn announcement: partner and OpsRadar both post.
- Goal: 2 paid clients by **Day 60**, partner moves to Certified path.

### Month 3: revenue review + tier qualification
- 30-min business review with founder.
- Review: clients closed, pipeline, blockers, NPS of referred customers, content opportunities.
- If at 3+ clients: promoted to Certified Partner, badge updated, next quarter's co-marketing asset scheduled.
- If at 1–2 clients: identify the blocker. (Usually one of: (a) wrong client segment, (b) discomfort with the close, (c) under-using the asset library.)

### Day-90 milestones
- 80% of partners have closed ≥1 client
- 50% of partners have closed ≥2 clients
- 20% of partners have hit Certified (3+)
- Average partner CAC (founder time + commission) tracked weekly

## 6. The Multi-Client Dashboard — PRD

**Status:** Mandatory unlock for the program. Cannot recruit partners without shipping this. Target ship date: Day 45 of Path-A plan (15 days before partner program launch).

### 6.1 Core requirement (the one-line spec)
> One partner login. All of their agency clients visible. Switching between clients takes one click. Cross-client portfolio metrics on the home view.

### 6.2 Information architecture

**Home view = Portfolio view (default landing for partners with ≥ 2 clients):**
- Hero row: 4 KPI tiles — Total ARR managed, Average client health score, Open alerts requiring partner action, Clients in red zone (< 60 health).
- Client list: card grid, one card per client. Each card: client logo, health score (0–100 with color), trailing-30-day delta, # open alerts, last alert timestamp, "Open" button.
- Sort/filter: by health, by ARR, by alerts, by recently active.
- "Add new client" CTA top-right (only enabled if partner has remaining seats in their tier or chooses to expand).

**Client view (the existing single-tenant OpsRadar dashboard):**
- Reached by clicking a client card OR via top-bar client switcher (dropdown, sticky, persistent across pages).
- Client switcher visually pins the active client (logo + name in top-left, always visible).
- "Back to portfolio" button always 1 click away.

### 6.3 Switching pattern decision
**Decision: persistent top-bar dropdown switcher + portfolio home.**
- Sidebar approach (Linear-style): rejected — eats horizontal space, scales poorly past 6 clients.
- List-page approach (Vercel teams): rejected — requires 2 clicks to switch, breaks flow during a client meeting.
- **Dropdown switcher with portfolio home as the "back" target** wins: 1 click to switch, ⌘K-searchable, scales to 20+ clients, easy to keep "active client" affordance prominent.

### 6.4 Per-client health-at-a-glance
- Single 0–100 health score per client, weighted: utilization (30%), project margin (30%), scope drift (20%), cash flow (10%), client churn risk (10%). Weights configurable in v2; locked in v1.
- Color bands: 80+ green, 60–79 amber, < 60 red.
- Trailing 30-day sparkline on the card.

### 6.5 Aggregate partner metrics (Portfolio KPI tiles)
1. **Total ARR managed** — sum of every client's MRR × 12, displayed as $ and trailing 30-day delta %.
2. **Average client health** — weighted by ARR (a $500K client outranks a $50K client).
3. **Open partner-action alerts** — alerts tagged "requires advisor action" across all clients (e.g., "Acme: 23% scope drift detected — recommend renegotiation").
4. **Clients in red zone** — count + names, click-through to each.

### 6.6 White-label / branded view (optional, off by default)
- Partner can upload their logo + accent color in Settings → Branding.
- This applies *only* to:
  - The PDF "Operations Brief" export (header carries partner logo + "Powered by OpsRadar" footer).
  - The shared client URL (`/client/{id}/share?token=`) used in COO ↔ agency QBR meetings.
- Does NOT apply to the partner's own login interface. (OpsRadar branding stays for our brand surface.)
- Does NOT apply to in-app emails sent to the agency. (Those stay OpsRadar-branded.)
- No custom domain hosting in v1. Maybe in v2 for Strategic Partners.

### 6.7 Permissions model

| Action | Partner can | Client (agency owner) can |
|---|---|---|
| View all dashboards, alerts, reports | Yes | Yes (own only) |
| Configure integrations | Yes (with client's stored OAuth tokens) | Yes |
| Trigger agentic actions (Pro tier) | Yes — with confirmation modal | Yes |
| Invite team members to the client account | Yes | Yes |
| Edit billing / change plan / cancel | **No** | Yes (only) |
| Add a payment method | **No** | Yes (only) |
| Export data / generate PDF | Yes | Yes |
| Configure alert thresholds | Yes | Yes |
| See cross-client benchmarks | Yes (portfolio aggregate) | No (only own data) |
| Remove themselves as advisor | Yes | n/a |
| Remove the partner from the account | n/a | Yes (kills attribution going forward, see §10.4) |

### 6.8 Notification routing
- **Default: both get alerts.** Partner gets all alerts (filtered into a daily digest by default; severity-based escalation for "red" alerts within 10 min). Client gets only alerts tagged "owner-actionable" (e.g., "Approve this invoice" — not "Scope drift detected on Project X" which is the COO's call).
- Per-client override: partner can configure "send all alerts only to me" for one client (e.g., a client who's drowning and wants advisor-only routing).
- Channels: in-app, email, Slack DM (if connected), no SMS in v1.
- Quiet hours: partner-side configurable (e.g., no notifications between 7pm–7am partner local time).

### 6.9 Build-vs-buy + sequencing
- This is built in-house. No SaaS dashboard product fits.
- Build sequence:
  1. Portfolio home page (3 days)
  2. Client switcher (1 day)
  3. Aggregate KPI tiles (2 days)
  4. Permissions model — add `partner_role` to existing accounts table (2 days)
  5. White-label PDF export + branding settings (3 days)
  6. Partner notification routing (2 days)
- **Total: ~13 dev days. 3-week buffer for testing & polish = ship by Day 45.**

## 7. Partner Tracking & Attribution

### 7.1 The 4-channel attribution model
We accept attribution via — in priority order:

1. **Unique referral link** (Rewardful-generated, format `opsradar.com/?via=partner-handle`). 60-day cookie window. Default for self-service signups.
2. **Promo code** (`OPSRADAR-[partner-initials]`). For partners doing podcast / webinar / spoken intros. Code applied at checkout grants attribution + a 5% first-3-months discount for the agency.
3. **Manual attribution at deal close** (founder-tagged in HubSpot or Stripe metadata). For partners who do warm intros that bypass tracking. Partner emails the founder "I'm sending you Sean from SwishAd — please tag me." Founder applies the partner attribution.
4. **Self-claim window** (partner portal): within 14 days of an agency signup, a partner can submit a claim ("I sourced [agency]") with proof (email forward, Slack DM screenshot). Founder reviews within 48h. Approved claims unlock attribution from signup.

### 7.2 When attribution gets credited
- **At paid conversion**, not at signup. We pay on revenue collected. Cookie/code/manual tag stores the attribution claim; commission ledger only fires when the first invoice is paid.
- **Partner sees signup credit in their portal immediately** (so they can track funnel), but earnings line item only appears once paid.

### 7.3 Self-service partner portal — what they see
- Earnings dashboard: paid this month, paid lifetime, next payout date, next payout amount.
- Funnel: clicks → signups → trials → paid (Rewardful native).
- Per-client: which agency, status, MRR, partner take.
- Asset library: slides, scripts, templates, demo videos.
- Education hub: certification refresher, new feature briefs (5-min videos).
- Settings: payout method, branding, tax docs (W-9/W-8).

### 7.4 Tool decision: Rewardful (with PartnerStack as the "after $200K MRR from partners" upgrade path)

| Tool | Year-1 monthly cost | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Rewardful** | $49–$149/mo. 0% transaction fee. Stripe-native. | **Winner for year 1.** Stripe-native sync, ships in a weekend, $49 base scales to $7.5K/mo affiliate-driven revenue. Partner portal is good enough. |
| PartnerStack | $800+/mo + 3–15% transaction fees | Wrong stage. Built for $1M+ ARR programs with dedicated partner teams. Re-evaluate when we cross $200K MRR from partners. |
| Tolt | $49+/mo. 0% transaction fee. Stripe+Paddle. | Solid Rewardful alternative. Pick Rewardful only because it's slightly more mature in the Stripe-SaaS niche; would not lose sleep choosing Tolt. |
| FirstPromoter | $59+/mo | Considered. Rewardful's Stripe sync edges it out. |
| Build it ourselves | ~3 dev-weeks | Rejected. Re-implementing payouts, tax forms, fraud controls is a bad use of solo-founder dev time. |

**Build internally:** the partner-facing multi-client dashboard (§6) and the founder-facing CRM tags (HubSpot). Buy: the affiliate engine and payouts (Rewardful).

### 7.5 Fraud / abuse guardrails
- Self-referral block: signup email cannot match partner contact email or domain (with allowlist for partner's own agency clients).
- Velocity cap: partners flagged if > 5 signups in 7 days (manual review required — fine for legit explosions, just want a human in the loop).
- 60-day clawback (see §3) handles "rage quit" agencies.
- All commission held for 30 days before payout (matches Stripe dispute window).

## 10. Risk & Edge Cases — Documented Decisions

### 10.1 Toxic partner / public criticism
**Risk:** A partner gets disgruntled (commission dispute, product complaint, personality conflict) and starts trashing OpsRadar in Fractionals United or on LinkedIn.

**Decision:**
- Partner agreement includes a 30-day "good faith resolution" clause — both parties agree to discuss before public escalation.
- If breached: program termination + commission stops on new deals (existing customers' commission continues per the lifetime promise — we don't punish their existing book).
- Don't sue. The PR cost of dragging a fractional COO through court is 100x the dollar amount in dispute.
- Founder responds publicly with a one-line acknowledgment + offer to resolve privately. Never argue in threads.

### 10.2 Partner brings a client we already had in pipeline
**Risk:** Partner submits "I'm bringing you Acme Agency" — but we've already had Acme in HubSpot for 3 weeks from a cold email.

**Decision:** **First-touch wins.** Whoever has the dated record first gets attribution. We document this in the agreement upfront.
- Exception: if our first-touch is a cold outbound and the partner has a closed-deal-quality warm relationship that's actually driving the close, founder can manually credit the partner. Discretionary; document the rationale in HubSpot.
- Transparency: partners can see "this prospect is already in our pipeline, claimed by [source/date]" via the portal's claim-check before they invest effort. Prevents the awkward "I worked this for 6 weeks and you stole it" scenario.

### 10.3 Partner leaves the fractional space (takes a full-time COO role)
**Risk:** Best partner gets a $200K full-time COO offer and accepts.

**Decision:**
- Their commission on existing clients continues (lifetime promise applies). They get paid forever, even retired.
- Their tier badge expires after 12 months of no new deals.
- Their full-time employer is automatically offered a free Pro account and an OpsRadar onboarding call.
- This is a feature, not a bug — fractional → in-house COO is the #1 career path, and a former partner who's now running ops at a 50-person agency is a great word-of-mouth source.

### 10.4 Partner wants to take "their" dashboard to a competitor
**Risk:** Partner who built their practice on OpsRadar's multi-client view says "I'm switching my clients to [competitor X]."

**Decision:**
- They can't take our product. Their clients' OpsRadar accounts belong to the clients — partner can be removed but the data stays with the agency.
- Their referral link is deactivated within 24h of partner program termination.
- Their commission on existing referred clients continues (lifetime; we don't pay malice, we pay contracts).
- Recourse: nothing legal. Build a better product. The dashboard is the moat — make the cost of switching real by ensuring the dashboard is 20%+ better than any alternative every quarter.

### 10.5 Partner wants white-label reseller terms
**Risk:** Partner says "I want to resell OpsRadar at $3K to my clients under my brand and keep $1.5K." (Effectively asking for 50% margin + white-label.)

**Decision: NO** in year 1. (See §2.7 for full rationale.)
- Founder-level conversation: "Our partner program is referral-based with 25% lifetime recurring. We don't offer reseller economics because [data sovereignty + brand + benchmark moat]. If those numbers don't work for you, I respect it — but I won't carve out a one-off and break the program for everyone else."
- Re-evaluate in 2027 when revenue + team can support a true channel ops function.

### 10.6 GDPR / privacy when a partner sees multiple clients' data
**Risk:** Partner has access to 4 agencies' internal financial + ops data. One agency's owner says "wait, what does my COO actually see?"

**Decision:**
- Partner access is **disclosed and consented** at agency-side onboarding: "Your fractional COO will have advisor-level access to view your operational data. They cannot make billing changes, cancel your account, or share data with any third party." Plain English. Required checkbox.
- Per-client toggle in agency owner settings: "Revoke advisor access" — one-click, immediate, no founder approval needed.
- Audit log: every partner view of client data is logged and viewable by the agency owner.
- Data Processing Addendum (DPA) signed by the partner as part of the agreement. Standard SaaS template, references our existing DPA with sub-processors.
- For EU clients (when we have them): the partner is a "sub-processor" in GDPR terms; flagged in the privacy policy.

### 10.7 Partner overcommits and stops responding
**Risk:** Partner signs the agreement, then ghosts. Doesn't onboard, doesn't refer.

**Decision:**
- 30-day check-in email: "Want to schedule your first client demo?"
- 60-day: friendly "Anything blocking you?" DM from founder.
- 90-day: program review — if 0 clients and 0 engagement, partner is moved to "Dormant" status (still has account, no badge, no benefits). No public removal.
- No penalty. Some fractional COOs evaluate for 6 months before activating. Patience compounds; rage doesn't.

### 10.8 Partner refers a customer who churns immediately (60–90 day cliff)
**Risk:** Partner brings 3 clients, all churn by month 4. Commission paid, customer revenue gone.

**Decision:**
- 60-day clawback (§3) handles month 1–2 churn.
- After 60 days: no clawback. Cost of customer churn is on us (it's a product/fit problem, not a partner problem).
- If a single partner's referred customers churn at > 50% trailing 90-day rate: founder calls. Likely cause: wrong ICP fit (partner is referring agencies outside our $500K–$5M ARR sweet spot). Tier review, retraining offered, no penalty.

### 10.9 Two partners both claim the same referred customer
**Risk:** Agency owner says "I heard about you from both COO A and COO B."

**Decision:**
- First-touch wins. The link, code, or manual tag with the earliest verifiable timestamp gets the attribution.
- If both are within 24h of each other and both have credible claims: founder splits commission 50/50, both partners notified.
- This is rare and tolerable — the cost of micro-policing is higher than the cost of occasional split commissions.

## 11. The One Decision That Determines Whether This Program Works

**The recurring commission % + lifetime vesting.** Not the tier structure. Not the dashboard UX (necessary but not sufficient). Not even recruitment volume.

Because: fractional COOs talk to each other. The first 5 partners will tell the next 50 what we pay. If we land on "25% recurring, lifetime, no cliff," Fractionals United becomes a self-replicating recruiting channel. If we land on "20% for 12 months," we're indistinguishable from HubSpot and we lose the word-of-mouth flywheel — which is the entire reason this channel beats every other.

The single decision: **commit to lifetime, commit to 25%, and never walk it back even when finance starts modeling the LTV drag.** The compounded effect of a partner-network endorsement is worth more than any model says.
