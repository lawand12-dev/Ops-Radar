## The One Structural Thing You Must Do First

**Incorporate outside Iraq before approaching any international investor.**

Iraqi company law requires 51% local ownership — this legally blocks most international VCs from taking equity in an Iraqi-registered entity. The standard move for Iraqi founders is to incorporate a holding company abroad and operate the Iraqi entity as a subsidiary. Details in Section 4 below.

## Section 1 — Local Iraq/Kurdistan Programs

### Five One Labs Incubator — Erbil + Sulaimani
fiveonelabs.org

The anchor startup support organization in Iraqi Kurdistan since 2017. Runs a coworking space in Sulaimani and a hub in Erbil.

- **Investment / funding**: Up to $6,000 seed prize at demo day (competition-based)
- **Equity**: None — grant/prize model
- **Stage**: Ideation to early MVP, pre-revenue is the default
- **Program**: 3 months, 100+ hours training, weekly CEO Talks, one-on-one advisory
- **Why first**: No equity dilution. Provides the credibility and network to access every other fund on this list. Five One Labs alumni have gone on to raise from IVP, Euphrates, and international sources.
- **Also relevant**: Five One Invest (fiveoneinvest.com) is their investor-matchmaking arm — engage this simultaneously

## Section 4 — Company Incorporation Strategy

### The Critical Problem: Iraq + International VC

Iraqi company law requires 51% local ownership. Mercury (the most startup-friendly US bank) explicitly lists Iraq as a prohibited country for beneficial owners. Wise does not operate in Iraq at all. **Without a foreign entity and UAE residency, you cannot open the banking accounts that investors need to wire money to.**

The solution is a two-phase approach:

---

### Phase 1 (Immediate): ADGM UAE Entity + Residency

**ADGM (Abu Dhabi Global Market) Tech Startup License** is the recommended first structure.

**Why ADGM over other UAE free zones:**
- Uses English common law natively — SAFEs and convertible notes are standard
- Most VC-recognized in MENA (Flat6Labs, 500 Global MENA, Wamda, MEVP all invest via ADGM structures)
- ADGM-to-Delaware flip is a well-documented, low-friction path for when US investors appear
- Enables UAE entrepreneur residence visa (2-year renewable)

**Cost breakdown (Year 1):**

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| ADGM Tech Startup License | USD 1,500/year |
| Dedicated desk / virtual office (required) | USD 5,000–6,000/year |
| UAE residence visa (govt fees + medical) | USD 1,000–1,500 |
| Bank account opening (no fee, deposit varies) | AED 10K–50K hold |
| **Total Year 1** | **~USD 8,000–9,000** |

**Banking options with UAE entity + residency:**

| Bank | Notes |
|---|---|
| Mashreq NeoBiz | Best for startups — fastest onboarding, lower minimums |
| RAKBANK | Good for SMEs, quick approval |
| Emirates NBD | Requires AED 50K minimum balance |
| ADIB (Islamic) | AED 10K minimum, Sharia-compliant |
| Mercury (via UAE address) | Accessible once you have UAE residency — Iraq is blocked, UAE is not |
| Airwallex / Wise Business | Available through UAE entity |

**Step-by-step:**
1. Visit UAE on tourist visa (Iraqi passport holders can get UAE visit visa)
2. Apply for ADGM Tech Startup License online at adgm.com
3. During UAE trip: finalize ADGM setup, open bank account (Mashreq NeoBiz), apply for residence visa
4. Once you have UAE address: apply to Mercury with UAE beneficial owner address
5. You now have: ADGM entity + UAE bank account + Mercury account — sufficient for all MENA VC conversations and pre-seed fundraising

---

### Phase 2 (When US investor is writing a check): Delaware C-Corp

**Incorporate a Delaware C-Corp** (via Doola ~$300 or Stripe Atlas ~$500) and make the ADGM entity a wholly-owned subsidiary. This is the "Delaware flip" — a documented standard for MENA founders.

- Do this ONLY when YC or a US VC is writing a check. Doing it earlier adds compliance overhead with no benefit
- **Form 5472**: Mandatory annual filing for any 25%+ foreign-owned US corp. Penalty for non-filing: $25,000. Budget $500–$1,500/year for accountant
- Iraq has no tax treaty with the US — 21% corporate tax + 30% dividend withholding tax applies if profits are taken out. On pre-revenue startup, this is irrelevant
- **Total Delaware ongoing cost**: ~$1,000–$2,000/year in compliance + $300 franchise tax

**What to avoid:**
- UK Ltd: Banking still a problem for Iraq-resident director; low VC-friendliness
- Estonian e-Residency: Useless for VC fundraising. Banks don't open accounts; no MENA/US VC recognition
- Cayman: Overkill and expensive at this stage
