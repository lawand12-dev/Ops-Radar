## Section 1 — Why we built this

**H2:** `Three problems, one product.`

**Section intro:**
`Every research report we ran came back with the same three customer complaints about every AI tool on the market. We built OpsRadar to answer all three at once.`

### Problem 1 — Tool sprawl

**The complaint, verbatim from a 15-person agency owner:**
`"15-person agency paying $5,200/month on 10 AI apps — more than two full-time salaries."` *— BizTech Magazine, 2025*

**Our answer:** `One workspace. AI employees, company brain, and 25+ integrations — replaces the ten apps.`

**The complaint, verbatim:**
`"Every AI user in 2026 has experienced the same moment: you explain your preferences, and the next session, the AI has forgotten everything."` *— Jenova.ai, May 2026*

**The complaint:** `Devin pursued impossible solutions for 6 hours that a human did in 36 minutes. 11x sent emails that tanked customer deliverability. AI SDR companies are churning at 70-80% in 90 days.`

**Our answer:** `Approval gate on every write. Per-tool autonomy. Nothing fires without your finger on it.`

## Section 3 — What we believe

**H2:** `Four convictions that shape every decision.`

### Conviction 1 — The brain is the asset. It's yours.

`When you sell your agency, the buyer pays for documented operations — 8-12× EBITDA instead of 5-9× (First Page Sage / Wayfront M&A data). The brain we help you build IS that documentation. It lives in your Notion. You can export it as a markdown zip from /dashboard/brain/download on day 1 of your trial. We don't hold your data hostage because the data IS what the buyer pays for. Helping you keep it is helping you exit at a higher multiple.`

### Conviction 2 — Earned autonomy beats blind autonomy

`The horror stories you've read about AI agents — sent the wrong email, billed the wrong customer, deleted the database — they all share one architecture: full autonomy on day one, no audit, no graduation. We chose the opposite. Every high-risk action stays in your queue forever (firing a client, sending invoices, mass email). Routine actions auto-fire only after you've approved them 30+ times at 90%+ rate. The agents EARN their autonomy. They don't get it for free. The product gets less impressive in a demo and more trustworthy in production.`

### Conviction 3 — Vertical wedge, universal architecture, multi-tenant leverage

`We sell to agencies first because they live on retainers, hire fractional COOs, and pay for ten AI tools that don't talk. But we have a second motion that compounds: fractional COOs running 5-10 agency clients each, on a multi-tenant version of the same product. Each FO is a 5-10× leveraged buyer. Q3 2026 we expand to home services (HVAC, plumbing, electrical). Q4, more verticals. The architecture is universal; the wedge is sharp.`

### Conviction 4 — Pricing should never surprise you

`No token billing. No premium-model multipliers. The only thing that bills above your tier is something you literally clicked Approve on. The 40% of customers Gartner says abandon agentic AI because of unforeseen variable costs? We made sure that's structurally impossible with us.`

## Section 4 — What we won't build

**H2:** `The honest list of things we said no to.`

**Section intro (24 words):**
`Every product is defined as much by what it isn't as by what it is. Here's what we deliberately won't ship.`

- `**Autonomous mode.** Every Devin / 11x post-mortem confirms this is where customer trust dies. We won't ship it, even when customers ask.`
- `**A workflow canvas (Lindy / Zapier UI).** The product is a chat. The chat compiles your description into the spec. The spec runs. Drag-and-drop visual builders are an artifact of a pre-AI era.`
- `**Token-credit pricing.** Lindy's stall at $5M ARR after $50M raised tells the whole story. We never charge for tokens.`
- `**More than 28 built-in agents.** Adding more isn't a wedge — it's feature bloat. The depth comes from the senior-COO playbook in each one, not the count.`
- `**Anything that holds your data hostage.** The Notion mirror, the export endpoint, the markdown-in-Postgres brain — they exist so cancellation can't take your knowledge with us.`
