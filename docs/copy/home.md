---

### OPERATE — runs the manual work

**One-line description:** The COO doesn't just spot the issue — it takes the next action with your approval (and a 30-minute undo window).

**Example action — human name, not code-style:**
*Overdue-invoice follow-up.* When an invoice ages past 30 days, the COO drafts the follow-up email in your voice, queues it in your sent box, and waits for your one-click approval. *(Live customer beta: average 4.2 invoices resurfaced per agency per month.)*

**Outcome:** Recovers **$3K–$12K/month** in cash flow that was previously waiting on the owner to remember.

---

### OBSERVE — sees the whole agency in one view

**One-line description:** One view across the 6–10 tools your team already lives in. Not a new place to log in — a real-time read on what's happening across them.

**Example action — human name:**
*Client health score.* Every Monday morning, the COO produces a 0–100 score per client based on margin, scope drift, utilization, and response cadence. Top 3 risks flagged with the specific dollar exposure.

**Outcome:** Catches the client you'd otherwise lose **73 days earlier** than your quarterly review. *(Detection lag benchmark: 30–90 days. We bring it to under 7.)*

---

### BRAIN — knows your agency

**One-line description:** Ask it anything about your business. It answers from your live data — not a generic LLM hallucinating about your team.

**Example action — human name:**
*Live margin chat.* Ask: "Which of our clients lost margin this quarter and why?" The COO answers in 4 seconds with the three clients, the dollar deltas, and the underlying reason — *Acme spent 47 unbilled hours on creative revisions in April.*

**Outcome:** Replaces **39.75 hours/month** the average strategist spends pulling these numbers (Fluency 2026). On a $80/hr loaded rate, that's **~$38K/year per strategist** back in billable work.

---

### GUARD — watches it while you sleep

**One-line description:** The night-shift COO. Watches your stack overnight and surfaces the things that need your attention before they become this week's fire.

**Example action — human name:**
*Pacing alert.* If a client's media spend trajectory will overshoot the monthly cap by Friday, the COO catches it Sunday night, drafts the client comms, and queues your decision: pause, top up, or rebalance.

**Outcome:** Reclaims your Sunday. Removes the **71% of campaigns at risk** (Fluency 2026) from your weekend mental load.

## How it works in 3 steps

**Section eyebrow:** `HOW IT WORKS`
**Headline:** `Three steps. Live in the time it takes to onboard a new client.`
**Subhead:** `No rip-and-replace. No 6-month implementation. Plugs into the stack you already pay for and starts watching the same week.`

---

**Step 1 — Connect (15 minutes)**

You give us read access to the 4–6 tools your agency lives in — HubSpot, ClickUp, Harvest, Stripe, your ad accounts. We use the integration model that fits each tool: customer-owned API keys where it matters most. **The tools you already pay for stay where they are. Nothing rips, nothing replaces.**

*Trust line under the step:* `Read-only by default. You approve every action before it sends. 30-minute undo on everything.`

---

**Step 2 — Watch (first 7 days)**

The COO spends the first week learning your agency — which clients are retainer vs project, where margins are healthy, where utilization is leaking, what "normal" looks like for your size. By day 7 you get your first weekly Health Score report — and your first 3 specific recommendations with dollar amounts attached.

*Trust line under the step:* `We do not store your client business data in our database. We live-fetch from your tools every time. Your data stays in your tools.`

---

**Step 3 — Act (week 2 onward)**

The COO starts recommending — and, where you've given it permission, acting. Drafts the variance invoice. Surfaces the unprofitable client. Queues the pacing alert. Every action sits in an approval queue with a 30-minute undo. You stay in charge. The COO does the watching.

*Trust line under the step:* `Most agencies see their first $1K+ recovery in week 3. The full payback runs ~14 months on benchmark — under 4 months for most of our beta agencies.`
