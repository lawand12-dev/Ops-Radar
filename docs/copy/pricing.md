## Risk-Reversal Block

> If OpsRadar isn't earning its keep in the first month, we send the money back the same day. Pro tier gets 60 days, because agentic actions take 2 to 4 weeks of trust before you turn them on for real clients.

**Why we don't promise "we'll find $X or it's free":** because the second we put a dollar guarantee on it, the audit becomes a contract we have to litigate every month. ## Pricing FAQ

*Use this section to populate the empty Pricing tab on `/faq` too. The objection bank is in `docs/pricing-depth.md` §8.*

### "$1,500 is steep for us."

If you compare $1,500 to a software subscription, yes — it's high. If you compare it to manual reporting time, the cost of finding an unprofitable client in month 3 instead of week 2, and the fractional COO you're either paying or thinking about hiring, it's not even close. Productive.io's 2026 data: 50% of agencies leave 20%+ of profit untapped to ops inefficiency. On a $2M agency at 20% margins, that's **$80K/year sitting in the spreadsheet.** OpsRadar Growth recovers a single-digit fraction of it and pays for itself 4x over. Tell us what you're paying now and we'll show you the math in five minutes. If the number isn't 3x what you'd pay us, don't sign.

### "Why is Pro 2x Growth?"

Because Pro replaces the part of a fractional COO that *acts*. Growth tells you what to do. Pro does it — invoice drafts, PM nudges, retainer adjustments, with a 30-minute undo on everything. That saves 30 to 40 hours a month of owner or head-of-ops time. At $250/hour, that's $7,500 to $10,000 of value. Pro is $3,000. The 2x ratio is conservative.

### "Can we do month-to-month?"

Yes. Every tier is month-to-month by default. Annual saves you 17% (2 months free). Most agencies start monthly, stay 3 months, then switch to annual when they're ready to lock the price.

### "We need to start at the lowest tier and prove it."

Starter is built for exactly that. The agencies that stay on Starter forever are running 3 tools and one or two clients — probably not you. Most agencies move to Growth in month 2 or 3 because per-client profitability pays for the upgrade twice over. We'll tell you when the math says move up.

### "Our fractional COO costs less than your Pro."

Which one? The market floor for a real fractional COO is $7,000/month. If yours is under $3,000, they're either brand new, doing this on the side, or both — meaning their knowledge walks out the door the moment they get a bigger client. If they're generating $40K of value a month, keep them. Run OpsRadar Starter alongside so they have live numbers instead of stale spreadsheets.

### "Do you discount?"

Annual prepay: 17% off (2 months free). Multi-year prepay: 22% year 1, 25% year 2, price-locked. Founder pricing: 50% off for 3 months, capped at the first 10 logos. That's it. We don't match competitor prices because we're not competing on price with a PSA — we're competing on outcome with a fractional COO.

### "What if I want to cancel?"

Email or message us. 30-day money-back, no questions asked. 60-day on Pro. After that, cancel any time at the end of your billing cycle. Your data was never trapped in us — it lives in HubSpot, ClickUp, Harvest, Stripe, etc. We were only reading from them.

### "Why isn't there a free tier?"

Because a free tier turns the founder into a free-tier support engineer, and the founder needs to ride the first 100 customers to capture what's actually working. Our free surface is the audit at `/audit` — three minutes, real dollar number, no credit card. Then a 30-minute call, then paid. Demo-to-paid runs 55–75% in this category, which is 6–8x what a free trial would do.
