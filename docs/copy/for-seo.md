## FAQ rows (5 SEO-specific — slot into the existing FAQ accordion pattern)

*(Voice: peer-to-peer, founder answering. No "Great question!" energy. Direct. Names what's in and out of scope.)*

**1. Do you replace Ahrefs / SEMrush, or augment them?**
We augment. Ahrefs and SEMrush are still the best at what they do — crawling, backlink data, keyword databases. We sit on top, read their APIs, and tell you what to do about the data. The AI COO is the operator layer; Ahrefs is the data layer. You keep paying for Ahrefs. We make sure your team stops manually opening it every Monday.

**2. How do you track LLM citations? What does that actually look like?**
We query ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity weekly with each client's top 20 branded and category prompts — the prompts a real buyer would type. We log which sites are cited in the response, compare to last week, and surface citation share vs. the 3 named competitors you flagged at onboarding. It's not perfect (LLM responses vary) and we average across 3 runs to denoise. The output is a weekly digest your client's CMO can read in 60 seconds.

**3. Can the AI COO write SEO content for our clients?**
No — and that's deliberate. Content briefs, content scoring, and the pipeline that gets a brief to a writer in 60 seconds: yes. The actual writing stays with your team. The AI COO is the operator, not the writer. If we ever ship content generation, it'll be a separate product. The COO is for ops; ops is what this page is about.

**4. We're a 5-person SEO agency. Are we too small?**
You're at the floor of our ICP, not under it. The Starter tier ($500/mo) is shaped for 5–10 person agencies running 8–15 retainer clients. Below 5 people you're likely a solo + contractor model, and the per-client digest pattern doesn't pay for itself yet. If you're 5 people and growing toward 10, the AI COO is the layer that lets you grow without hiring an ops lead first.

**5. Are AI Overviews and LLM citations actually killing organic traffic, or is this hype?**
Both real, both measured. Ahrefs (Dec 2025): a 58% lower CTR for top pages when AIO appears. ALM Corp 2026: Position 1 loses 18% CTR, Position 2 loses 39%. Zero-click searches are now 60% of Google traffic (77% on mobile, 93% in AI Mode). Healthcare and finance YMYL pages are taking the hardest hits because AIO dominates those queries. LLM citations are newer, harder to measure, and the floor — not the ceiling — is "your client's category gets a 200-word answer with 4 citations, and you're not one of them." We don't sell hype. We sell tracking. If your clients aren't losing traffic to AI yet, the digest tells you the same thing — and you get the win of telling them, "we're tracking it, and you're fine."
