# Horizontal "AI Workforce + Company Brain" — Evidence Report

## The companies, by the numbers

**1. Glean — the only clear horizontal $10M+ ARR winner.** $200M ARR (Dec 2025), doubled in 9 months, 50+ industries, $7.2B valuation, started mid-market tech and moved upmarket. Explicitly horizontal — "one horizontal platform" is their own framing. Crucially, **enterprise-only pricing** — six-figure floors, not SMB. [Glean $200M ARR press](https://www.glean.com/press/glean-surpasses-200m-in-arr-for-enterprise-ai-doubling-revenue-in-nine-months), [Sacra Glean at $200M](https://sacra.com/research/glean-at-200m-arr/).

**2. Hebbia — pseudo-horizontal, but de-facto vertical.** $13M ARR (June 2024, no fresher number public), $700M valuation. Sells "horizontally" across finance/legal/consulting but **33% of customers are top global asset managers** — that's a vertical in disguise. Pricing $3K–$10K+/user/year enterprise. [Sacra Hebbia](https://sacra.com/c/hebbia/), [Contrary Research](https://research.contrary.com/company/hebbia).

**3. Lindy — the closest analog to OpsRadar's pitch, and a warning.** Founded 2023, **$5.1M ARR by 2024**, ~$50M raised. Explicitly horizontal SMB "AI employees." Founder Flo Crivello publicly repositioned to unlock 7-figure growth, suggesting horizontal-pure was not enough. [Latka Lindy revenue](https://getlatka.com/companies/lindyai), [SaaS Club podcast on repositioning](https://saasclub.io/podcast/lindy-flo-crivello-450/).

**4. Notion AI — horizontal, $600M ARR, but it's a doc tool with AI bolted on, not "AI workforce."** 100M users, 21,000 Custom Agents in beta, 2,800 internal agents. Custom Agents only launched Feb 2026 — too new to count as proof of horizontal workforce thesis. [Notion 3.3 release](https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-02-24), [TechCrunch on agents](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/13/notion-just-turned-its-workspace-into-a-hub-for-ai-agents/).

**5. Microsoft Copilot 365 — 20M paid seats, but it's a writing assistant in practice.** April 2026 milestone. SMB Business tier $18/user/mo. Not "hire AI employees" — it's "AI in Word/Excel/Teams." [WindowsNews 20M seats](https://windowsnews.ai/article/microsoft-365-copilot-hits-20m-paid-seats-enterprise-ai-adoption-governance-roi.415952).

**6. Onyx (Danswer) — open-source, no public commercial ARR signal.** Strong GitHub footprint, MIT licensed, enterprise tier exists. No ARR disclosure found. [Onyx GitHub](https://github.com/onyx-dot-app/onyx), [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/danswer).

**7. YC W26/S26 — horizontals exist but tiny.** Corvera (W26) is horizontal-adjacent but **CPG back-office vertical** ($33K MRR in 4 weeks). No funded "horizontal AI workforce for any business" at scale. [Corvera YC page](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/corvera), [W26 batch](https://www.thevccorner.com/p/yc-w26-batch-complete-company-database).

**8. BOND (YC X25) — direct competitor, $3M seed, no ARR disclosed.** Same "AI Chief of Staff" horizontal pitch. Tiny so far. [BOND YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/bond).

**9. a16z Big Ideas 2026 — explicitly bets vertical-with-multiplayer-coordination, not pure horizontal.** "Healthcare, legal, and housing companies reached $100M+ ARR within a few years." Horizontal coordination layer matters, but domain depth is the wedge. [a16z Big Ideas Part 1](https://a16z.com/newsletter/big-ideas-2026-part-1/).

**10. "AI workforce + company brain" exact two-pillar pitch.** Hyper and Cignara use the framing but no ARR signal. ## The four questions, answered

**Q1: Single $10M+ ARR horizontal "AI workforce + brain" company today?**
**Yes — exactly one: Glean ($200M ARR).** Notion AI ($600M ARR) qualifies only if you accept docs-with-AI as "workforce + brain." No SMB-priced horizontal winner exists at $10M+ ARR. [Glean press](https://www.glean.com/press/glean-surpasses-200m-in-arr-for-enterprise-ai-doubling-revenue-in-nine-months).

**Q2: Why does Glean work despite "vertical wins" consensus?**
Three reasons evident from Sacra's breakdown: (a) **enterprise-only pricing** floors at ~$60K means each customer covers ~1,000 SMB seats of effort; (b) **100+ connectors moat** that took years to build — the integration layer IS the vertical depth; (c) **moved upmarket fast** — started mid-market tech, now Fortune 500 across 50 industries. Horizontal works when you can charge enterprise prices.

**Q3: If no SMB-priced horizontal $10M+ winner exists — open wedge or impossible?**
**Mostly (a) impossible at SMB price points, with a narrow (b) opening.** Lindy ($5M after $50M and 3 years), BOND (seed-stage), Corvera (had to pick CPG) all suggest horizontal-SMB hits a CAC/payback wall. The a16z thesis explicitly bets against it. The narrow opening: **integration-count moat at SMB price** (25→500+ Composio tools) — no one has demonstrably won there, but no one has clearly lost either. That's "unproven," not "open and unattacked."

**Q4: OpsRadar honest probability of $10M ARR in 18 months, staying horizontal at SMB-mid?**
**Honest range: 3–8%.** Reference class: Lindy hit $5M in 3 years with $50M and a top founder. BOND is at seed. Glean needed enterprise pricing to escape. To hit $10M in 18 months at, say, $500/mo ACV, OpsRadar needs ~1,670 paying customers — that's mid-market SaaS scale, not seed-stage reality. Adjusting upward for Composio breadth + strong agent stack: still <10%. Adjusting downward for the empirical pattern that every horizontal SMB AI workforce player has so far stalled or pivoted: closer to 3–5%.

**The honest read:** the vertical-wins consensus has empirical support. Glean is the existence proof horizontal CAN work, but only at enterprise prices. No one has solved horizontal-at-SMB-prices yet. That's a real "open" question, not an "open wedge." Staying horizontal is a bet that integration-count + Notion-brain ownership beats domain depth — a bet no one has won yet.
