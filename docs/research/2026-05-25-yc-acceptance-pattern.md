## Q1 — Shape of the YC-accepted pitch

Two templates dominate. Quotes pulled verbatim from YC pages:

1. **"AI [role] for [customer]"** — BOND: *"Your AI Chief of Staff"*; Corvera: *"AI agent workforce for CPG back-office"*; Veriad: *"AI compliance officers"*; General Legal: *"AI-native law firm with same-day turnaround"*; Rankai: *"AI-Native Agency for Organic Growth (SEO & GEO)"*.
2. **"Full-stack AI [profession]"** — Balance: *"Full-Stack AI Accounting"*; Cranston AI: *"Full Stack AI Accounting Firm"*; Manicule: *"AI-native technical documentation agency"*.

Tight, role-replacement, one industry, no platform-speak. ([Omnius breakdown](https://www.omnius.so/blog/agency-business-model))

## Q2 — Which OpsRadar positioning maps to a funded YC category?

- **(a) AI Chief of Staff for marketing agencies** — STRONGEST FIT. This is BOND's exact template ("AI Chief of Staff for [vertical]"). BOND went horizontal (CEOs); the agency-vertical wedge is open and matches YC's "AI [role] for [vertical]" pattern. ([BOND](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/bond))
- **(c) Vertical AI workforce for services businesses** — also fits, mirroring Corvera ("AI agent workforce for CPG"). Phrasing should be "AI [role] workforce for [vertical]."
- **(b) Customer-owned company brain** — fits the **stated RFS** (Blomfield S26 RFS #4), not yet a funded company. High-conviction angle but unproven traction-wise. ([YC RFS](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs))
- **(d) Approval-gated control surface** — exists as infra plays (Sentrial, Janus) but those sell to AI-builders, not to agency owners. Doesn't map cleanly.
- **(e) "28 agents with senior-COO playbook"** — no YC analog. Reads as feature depth, not a pitch. YC accepts *role*, not *count*.

Best one-liner shape for OpsRadar: **"AI Chief of Staff for marketing agencies"** (BOND template + vertical wedge) or **"AI COO for services businesses"**.

## Q3 — Public rejection / feedback patterns

Direct AI-agent rejection threads from 2025-2026 are sparse — YC sends 1-2 sentences only, not public. ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=15566081)) The repeated public signal from YC partners + RFS authors is the inverse — what they *want*:
- **Full-stack > tool-for-X.** Don't sell software to law firms; *be* the law firm. ([Omnius](https://www.omnius.so/blog/agency-business-model))
- **Domain knowledge is the moat**, not the model — Blomfield explicitly: *"the blocker is domain knowledge."* ([VC Corner](https://www.thevccorner.com/p/yc-summer-2026-requests-for-startups-ideas))
- W26 funded **vertical agents over horizontal platforms**, then layered infra (auth/observability) under them. ([redreamality](https://redreamality.com/blog/yc-w26-ai-agents-replace-saas/))

Implication: the "agency-vertical AI COO" angle is on-trend; the "platform with 28 agents" framing is off-trend.
