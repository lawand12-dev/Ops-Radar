# VC & Funding Guide — Pre-Revenue Startup from Iraq/Kurdistan

> Last updated: May 2026  
> Context: Pre-revenue, Iraq/Kurdistan-based founder

---

## The honest reality first

Most traditional VCs will not lead a cold pre-revenue deal from Iraq. This is not about your idea — it is about risk-adjusted geography, legal setup, and exit pathways. The right approach is a **staged ladder**: local incubators → MENA accelerators → global accelerators → VC. Each step gives you the proof and network that unlocks the next.

---

## Tier 1 — Start here (local, free or low-cost, no or minimal equity)

These programs exist specifically for Kurdistan/Iraq founders. Use them to get your first validation, mentors, and introductions.

### Five One Labs (Erbil + Sulaimani)
- **What**: Startup incubator and ecosystem builder, headquartered in Kurdistan Region of Iraq since 2017. Co-working space in Sulaimani + hub in Erbil.
- **Why it matters**: Specifically designed for Iraqi/Kurdish founders. Alumni have gone on to raise from international sources. Strong local network.
- **Website**: [fiveonelabs.org](https://fiveonelabs.org/)

### Orange Corners Kurdistan
- **What**: Dutch-funded startup incubator helping entrepreneurs from the Kurdistan Region launch and grow businesses. Provides training, mentorship, and market access.
- **Why it matters**: Free program, no equity taken, international network from the Netherlands.
- **Website**: [orangecorners.com/country/kurdistan-region-of-iraq](https://www.orangecorners.com/country/kurdistan-region-of-iraq/)

---

## Tier 2 — First institutional capital (MENA-wide, pre-revenue or early traction)

Once you have a prototype or early users, these programs can provide $50K–$500K.

### Flat6Labs ⭐ Best first institutional bet
- **What**: MENA's leading seed accelerator. Runs programs in Cairo, Jeddah, Abu Dhabi, Beirut, Tunis, Manama, Amman. 100+ startups funded annually.
- **What they invest**: Up to ~$65K via their Anava Seed Fund for ~10–15% equity, plus 4-month intensive program with mentors and demo day.
- **Why it matters**: Accepts pre-revenue startups. Largest institutional MENA network. Demo days bring in later-stage VCs.
- **Website**: [flat6labs.com/programs](https://flat6labs.com/programs/)

### Iraq Venture Partners (IVP)
- **What**: First Iraq-based VC firm. The IVP Catalyst Fund invests in Iraqi startups at seed stage.
- **Gateway Fund** (in progress): Will target pre-Series A and Series A, tickets $250K–$1M.
- **Why it matters**: They understand the local context, have boots on the ground, and actively support Iraqi startups at international events (Web Summit, STEP Dubai).
- **Website**: [iraqventurepartners.com](https://www.iraqventurepartners.com/)
- **Note**: Contact them directly — they are active in ecosystem building even outside formal fund cycles.

### Iraq Tech Ventures
- **What**: Acts as both seed funder and matchmaker, connecting international investors with promising Iraqi tech projects.
- **Website**: Listed on [iraqtech.io](https://iraqtech.io/)

### Arzan Venture Capital
- **What**: Kuwait-born VC, investing in MENA startups since 2014. Portfolio of 30+ startups. Backs early-stage tech teams.
- **Why it matters**: One of the most established early-stage MENA funds, willing to go in at pre-Series A.

## Tier 3 — Global accelerators (best career-changing outcomes)

### Y Combinator ⭐ Holy grail
- **What**: World's top accelerator. $500K for 7% equity. 3-month program in San Francisco. Demo Day to 3,000+ investors.
- **Iraqi/MENA track record**: Lezzoo (first Iraqi YC startup, 2019), Munify (MENA neobank, YC S2025). By 2022, ~50% of YC companies were international.
- **Why it fits**: YC does not require revenue. They fund at idea stage if the founder is exceptional. Being Iraqi is not a disqualifier — it can be a differentiator if you're solving a real local problem with global potential.
- **What you need**: A co-founder (YC strongly prefers 2-person teams), a working prototype, and a clear "why now / why you" story.
- **Apply**: [ycombinator.com/apply](https://www.ycombinator.com/apply)

### Antler ⭐ Best for truly pre-product/pre-revenue
- **What**: World's most active early-stage investor (1,520+ companies). Backs founders from inception — before you even have a co-founder. Residency programs in many cities.
- **Why it fits**: Antler is the rare investor that will back you before you have a product, revenue, or even a team. They match co-founders, provide pre-seed capital, and run a 10-week program.
- **Check sizes**: $100K–$200K pre-seed, equity around 10%.
- **Website**: [antler.co](https://www.antler.co/)

### 500 Global — Sanabil MENA Accelerator
- **What**: 12-week program for tech startups with early traction ready to expand in MENA.
- **Notes**: Program fee of $35K applies (it comes from the investment they make). Designed for founders ready to scale in MENA, not early-early stage.
- **Website**: [500.co/founders/mena/seed-accelerator](https://500.co/founders/mena/seed-accelerator)

## Tier 4 — Impact / development finance (underrated path)

### IFC (World Bank Group)
- The IFC has committed to investing up to $225M in startups across Africa, the Middle East, Central Asia, and Pakistan via its VC platform. Iraqi startups solving real economic problems (fintech, logistics, agri, healthcare) are exactly the type they back.
- Not a direct application — connect through Flat6Labs or IVP alumni who have IFC relationships.

## Recommended action plan (in order)

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Apply to Five One Labs or Orange Corners | Now — free, no equity |
| 2 | Build a working MVP / get first users | 0–3 months |
| 3 | Apply to Flat6Labs | Next open cohort |
| 4 | Contact Iraq Venture Partners directly | Parallel to step 2–3 |
| 5 | Apply to Antler or Y Combinator | Once you have co-founder + prototype |
| 6 | Apply to 500 Global MENA | Once you have early traction |

---

## One structural thing you must do early

**Incorporate outside Iraq** before approaching any international VC. Most global investors cannot legally invest into an Iraqi entity. The standard setup is:

- **Delaware C-Corp** (for US/YC/global investors) or  
- **UAE Free Zone company** (DIFC, ADGM, or mainland Dubai — for MENA investors)

Iraq Venture Partners and Five One Labs can advise on the best structure for your specific situation. Do not wait until an investor asks — set it up proactively.
