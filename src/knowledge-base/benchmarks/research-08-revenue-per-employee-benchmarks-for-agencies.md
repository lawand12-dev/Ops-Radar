## Key Facts

### 1. Profitable marketing agencies achieve $200,000-$300,000+ revenue per employee annually, while struggling agencies fall below $150,000

**Context:** The gap between winners and losers is roughly 2x on RPE
**Use Case:** Agency profitability diagnostic in audits
**Source:** Promethean Research 2024-2025 + TimeTackle benchmarks
**profitable rpe:** $200k-$300k+
**struggling rpe:** Below $150k

### 2. US marketing agencies averaged $220,000 revenue per employee in 2025, up 11% year-over-year, driven largely by AI automation adoption

**Context:** AI tools cutting operational costs is the primary driver of the RPE increase
**Use Case:** Positioning automation as the driver of profitability
**Source:** Promethean Research 2025 Digital Agency Industry Report
**average rpe:** $220,000
**yoy growth:** 11%
**primary driver:** AI automation

### 3. Mid-size marketing agencies (50-200 staff) average $172,000 RPE — the highest RPE optimization typically comes from this size range using retainer models

**Context:** Mid-size agencies hit the sweet spot of scale and efficiency
**Use Case:** Identifying optimal scale for agency operations
**Source:** AgencyAnalytics + Promethean benchmarks
**mid size avg rpe:** $172,000

### 4. Recruiting agencies show RPE ranging from $100,000-$150,000 (struggling) to $175,000-$250,000 (profitable), with top performers reaching $300,000

**Context:** Placement-based revenue models create more volatility than retainer-based marketing
**Use Case:** Recruiting agency benchmark comparison
**Source:** Recruiting industry benchmark analyses
**struggling:** $100k-$150k
**profitable:** $175k-$250k
**top performers:** $300k

### 5. Design and creative agencies average $100,000-$175,000 (struggling) to $150,000-$200,000 (profitable), with top performers at $250,000-$300,000

**Context:** Project-based overhead drags creative agency RPE below marketing averages
**Use Case:** Design agency RPE benchmarking
**Source:** Promethean Research + industry analyses
**struggling:** $100k-$175k
**profitable:** $150k-$200k
**top performers:** $250k-$300k

### 6. Automation adoption boosts agency efficiency by 20-30%, directly translating to higher revenue per employee

**Context:** The single biggest correlate with high-RPE agencies is automation infrastructure
**Use Case:** Core automation ROI argument for audit prospects
**Source:** Promethean Research 2024-2025
**efficiency boost:** 20-30%

### 7. Agency specialization drives a 15-25% revenue premium compared to generalist agencies

**Context:** Niche focus commands higher prices and deeper client relationships
**Use Case:** Specialization as a revenue lever
**Source:** Promethean Research + industry analyses
**specialization premium:** 15-25%

### 8. Retainer pricing models generate stable $220,000+ RPE, versus project-based models averaging only $150,000

**Context:** Pricing model alone accounts for a ~47% RPE difference between retainer and project agencies
**Use Case:** Business model conversion pitch — retainer advantage
**Source:** Industry benchmark analyses 2024-2025
**retainer rpe:** $220,000+
**project rpe:** $150,000
**difference:** ~47%

### 9. Lean agency structures achieve 60-70% billable utilization, compared to industry averages closer to 50%

**Context:** Billable utilization is the primary lever between struggling and profitable agencies
**Use Case:** Utilization improvement through ops automation
**Source:** Agency productivity benchmarks
**lean utilization:** 60-70%
**average utilization:** ~50%

### 10. The four strongest correlates with high revenue per employee are: (1) automation adoption, (2) service specialization, (3) retainer pricing model, (4) lean team structure

**Context:** Complete framework for diagnosing why an agency's RPE is below benchmark
**Use Case:** Profitability diagnostic framework for audits
**Source:** Synthesized from Promethean, AgencyAnalytics, and industry reports
**correlates:** Automation adoption, Specialization, Retainer pricing, Lean team structure
