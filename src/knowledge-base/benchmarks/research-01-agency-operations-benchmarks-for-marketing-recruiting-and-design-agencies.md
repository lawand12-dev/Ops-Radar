## Key Facts

### 1. Recruiters spend 14.6 hours per week searching for candidates

**Context:** Weighted average across surveyed recruiting agencies globally
**Use Case:** Recruiting agency pain point quantification
**Source:** [Bullhorn GRID 2025 Industry Trends Report](https://www.bullhorn.com/uk/grid/2025-industry-trends-report/)

### 2. Recruiting agencies expect AI to save 17 hours per week per recruiter

**Context:** Breakdown: 4.5h candidate search, 3.7h recruiter admin, 3.6h screening/evaluation, 2.7h winning new business, 2.4h submitting candidates
**Use Case:** Recruiting agency ROI calculation and automation opportunity sizing
**Source:** [Bullhorn GRID 2025 Industry Trends Report](https://www.bullhorn.com/uk/grid/2025-industry-trends-report/)

### 3. Recruiting agency automation adoption rates by workflow: 56% automate search/match, 51% automate recruiter administrative tasks, 42% automate screening/evaluation, 40% automate winning new business, 33% automate submitting candidates, 30% automate payroll/billing

**Context:** Reveals where manual operational load typically persists in recruiting stacks
**Use Case:** Benchmarking recruiting agency maturity and identifying automation gaps
**Source:** [Bullhorn GRID 2025 Industry Trends Report](https://www.bullhorn.com/uk/grid/2025-industry-trends-report/)

### 4. Most marketing agencies spend 15-30 minutes creating a client report in AgencyAnalytics, with 22% spending under 15 minutes

**Context:** Based on 2024 survey of 251 marketing agencies
**Use Case:** Marketing agency reporting automation benchmark
**Source:** [AgencyAnalytics 2024 Marketing Agency Benchmarks Report](https://assets.ctfassets.net/dfcvkz6j859j/sg2Zw7py7NgEVmFUaeV4e/bf4a8c0c0d085b3f3d80b8d55eb51937/AgencyAnalytics-2024-Benchmarks-Report.pdf)

### 5. 57% of agency clients prefer static reports with scheduled meetings, while 49% value live dashboard access

**Context:** Indicates most agencies need both structured reporting AND live visibility — automation opportunity
**Use Case:** Client reporting automation positioning
**Source:** [AgencyAnalytics 2024 Marketing Agency Benchmarks Report (n=251)](https://assets.ctfassets.net/dfcvkz6j859j/sg2Zw7py7NgEVmFUaeV4e/bf4a8c0c0d085b3f3d80b8d55eb51937/AgencyAnalytics-2024-Benchmarks-Report.pdf)

### 6. 80% of agency teams use real-time internal dashboards for campaign tracking

**Context:** Widespread dashboard adoption but still manual reporting to clients indicates gap
**Use Case:** Identifying reporting automation opportunities
**Source:** [AgencyAnalytics 2024 Marketing Agency Benchmarks Report (n=251)](https://assets.ctfassets.net/dfcvkz6j859j/sg2Zw7py7NgEVmFUaeV4e/bf4a8c0c0d085b3f3d80b8d55eb51937/AgencyAnalytics-2024-Benchmarks-Report.pdf)

### 7. Agencies allocate reporting time as follows: 30% to analysis, 20% to explaining data, 18% to recommendations

**Context:** Implies that 68% of reporting time is non-analytical work that is automatable
**Use Case:** Quantifying manual reporting waste for audit reports
**Source:** [Databox State of Agency-Client Collaboration 2024](https://databox.com/state-of-agency-client-collaboration)

### 8. 46% of agency leaders cite communication and transparency as a top client retention strategy

**Context:** Communication workflow automation directly impacts retention
**Use Case:** Positioning automation as a retention tool
**Source:** [AgencyAnalytics 2024 Marketing Agency Benchmarks Report (n=251)](https://assets.ctfassets.net/dfcvkz6j859j/sg2Zw7py7NgEVmFUaeV4e/bf4a8c0c0d085b3f3d80b8d55eb51937/AgencyAnalytics-2024-Benchmarks-Report.pdf)

### 9. 69% of agency leaders cite project and task management as critically important

**Context:** Project management is the #1 operational priority for agency owners
**Use Case:** Prioritizing PM automation opportunities in audits
**Source:** [AgencyAnalytics 2024 Marketing Agency Benchmarks Report (n=251)](https://assets.ctfassets.net/dfcvkz6j859j/sg2Zw7py7NgEVmFUaeV4e/bf4a8c0c0d085b3f3d80b8d55eb51937/AgencyAnalytics-2024-Benchmarks-Report.pdf)

### 10. Annual revenue per employee benchmarks for professional services firms: $147,000 (under 10 employees), $154,000 (10-30 employees), $153,000 (31-100 employees)

**Context:** 2024 results from the Professional Services Maturity Benchmark
**Use Case:** Hourly rate and labor arbitrage calibration for audits
**Source:** [Kantata/SPI Research 2025 Professional Services Maturity Benchmark](https://get.kantata.com/rs/677-LEJ-696/images/2025-ps-maturity-benchmark.pdf)

### 11. Professional services average EBITDA margin is 9.5%, implying operating costs are approximately 90.5% of revenue

**Context:** Baseline for identifying agencies in operational debt
**Use Case:** Ops cost ratio benchmark in audit benchmarks section
**Source:** [Kantata/SPI Research 2025 Professional Services Maturity Benchmark](https://get.kantata.com/rs/677-LEJ-696/images/2025-ps-maturity-benchmark.pdf)

### 12. Agency management tool adoption: 46.8% use integrated agency management tools, 45.5% use fragmented tools, 7.7% rely on spreadsheets

**Context:** Nearly half of agencies operate on fragmented stacks — the primary automation opportunity
**Use Case:** Stack maturity assessment and tool consolidation pitch
**Source:** [Productive 2024 Report on Agency Valuations (n=783)](https://productive.io/the-2024-report-on-agency-valuations/)

### 13. Agencies above 10 full-time employees need better knowledge storage and transfer systems, and more detailed operational metrics

**Context:** The 10-employee threshold is the scaling wall where manual operations start breaking down
**Use Case:** Identifying the scaling wall for audit urgency framing
**Source:** [Promethean Research 2025 Digital Agency Industry Report](https://prometheanresearch.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-Promethean-Research-Digital-Agency-Industry-Report-V1.02.pdf)

### 14. 62% of marketing agencies monitor 3-5 data sources per client and 45% of clients prioritize only 1-5 KPIs

**Context:** Limited KPI focus but multiple data sources means manual data aggregation is the bottleneck
**Use Case:** Reporting automation scope sizing
**Source:** [AgencyAnalytics 2024 Marketing Agency Benchmarks Report (n=251)](https://assets.ctfassets.net/dfcvkz6j859j/sg2Zw7py7NgEVmFUaeV4e/bf4a8c0c0d085b3f3d80b8d55eb51937/AgencyAnalytics-2024-Benchmarks-Report.pdf)

### 15. 74% of agency clients value data visualization in reporting

**Context:** Visual-first reporting is the expected standard
**Use Case:** Reporting automation quality standards
**Source:** [AgencyAnalytics 2024 Marketing Agency Benchmarks Report (n=251)](https://assets.ctfassets.net/dfcvkz6j859j/sg2Zw7py7NgEVmFUaeV4e/bf4a8c0c0d085b3f3d80b8d55eb51937/AgencyAnalytics-2024-Benchmarks-Report.pdf)
