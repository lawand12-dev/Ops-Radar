**Context:** Manual execution exposes client budgets to financial risk
**Use Case:** Risk-based urgency framing for campaign automation
**Source:** Fluency 2026 AdOps Benchmark

### 3. 36.99% of recruitment agencies cite 'too much manual work' as their biggest operational challenge

**Context:** Manual CRM updates, scheduling, and communication coordination dominate recruiter time
**Quote:** "Too much manual work remains the biggest operational challenge"
**Use Case:** Recruiting agency pain quantification
**Source:** Atlas Recruitment Industry Report 2024

**Use Case:** Tool fragmentation and integration opportunity identification
**Source:** Kantata State of Operations benchmark

**Source:** Reddit r/agency practitioner reports
