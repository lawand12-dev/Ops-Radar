**The pain — and why it's so acute**

Every agency founder thinks they know their profitable clients. They're wrong. Here's why:

Client A pays $10k/month. Feels great. But your team spends 120 hours on them. At your $85/hr blended cost, that's $10,200 in cost. You're losing money on your "best" client.

Client B pays $3k/month. Feels small. But it's a 4-hour/month retainer with no scope creep, no late-night calls, payments always on time. That's 85% margin.

Every agency owner has this situation. None of them know it clearly. They feel it vaguely — "Client A is exhausting" — but they never see the actual number. So they keep the wrong clients, fire the wrong clients, and underprice new ones the same way.

---

**What OpsRadar already has to solve this**

This isn't a new build. You already have:
- **Stripe** → revenue per client, payment history, overdue invoices
- **Harvest** → hours per client, by team member

The data is there. What's missing is a single view that combines them.

---

**What the feature looks like**

A **Client Profitability table** — one row per client, showing:

| Client | Revenue/mo | Hours | Cost | Margin | Payment | Trend |
|--------|-----------|-------|------|--------|---------|-------|
| Acme Co | $10,000 | 120h | $10,200 | **-2%** 🔴 | On time | ↓ |

| Gamma | $6,500 | 45h | $3,825 | **41%** 🟢 | On time | ↑ |

When an agency founder sees this for the first time — especially that red row — it's a gut punch. That's the aha moment. That's what they screenshot and post.

1. **Low barrier to entry** — connect 2 tools (Stripe + Harvest), get value immediately. No week-long setup. No team buy-in required.

4. **It's searchable** — "how do I track profit per client agency", "agency profitability tracking", "how to know if clients are profitable" — real searches with real intent.

5. **It's a Trojan horse** — once they're connected for this, OpsRadar can expand into the full dashboard, the AI briefings, all of it. The profitability table is just the door.
