## Key Facts

### 1. Theory of Constraints treats agency operations as a chain where throughput is limited by a single primary bottleneck — fixing everything else is waste. Only the constraint matters

**Context:** The core TOC insight applied to agencies
**Use Case:** Focus principle for operational audits
**Source:** Goldratt Theory of Constraints
**principle:** One constraint limits the whole system

### 2. The 5-step TOC framework (POOGI - Process of Ongoing Improvement): (1) Identify the constraint, (2) Exploit it without spending, (3) Subordinate everything else to it, (4) Elevate it with investment only if needed, (5) Repeat as constraint shifts

**Context:** The complete 5-step methodology
**Use Case:** Complete framework for agency audits
**Source:** Goldratt POOGI methodology
**steps:** Identify the constraint, Exploit without spending, Subordinate everything, Elevate with investment, Repeat as it shifts

### 3. The agency constraint is always the slowest step with the longest backlog and OTIF (On-Time In-Full) below 80% — this is how you find THE bottleneck objectively

**Context:** Quantified method for bottleneck identification
**Use Case:** Bottleneck detection methodology
**Source:** TOC applied to agencies
**detection metric:** OTIF <80% + longest backlog
