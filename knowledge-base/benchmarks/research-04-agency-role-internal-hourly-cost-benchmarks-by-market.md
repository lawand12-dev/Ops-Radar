### 13. Full internal hourly cost is typically 1.5x to 2x base salary when including benefits, taxes, overhead, and non-billable time

**Context:** Critical formula for accurate labor arbitrage calculations in audits
**Use Case:** Labor arbitrage math validation in audit generation
**Source:** Industry standard calculation methodology
**formula:** Base salary / 2000 hours × 1.5 to 2.0 overhead multiplier

### 14. Client billing rates typically run 2-3x the internal cost of the role delivering the work

**Context:** The gap between internal cost and billing rate defines agency gross margin
**Use Case:** Revenue impact modeling for automation opportunities
**Source:** Agency pricing benchmarks
**formula:** Billing rate = internal cost × 2 to 3
