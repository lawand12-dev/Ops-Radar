# Hours Wasted on Manual Work by Operational Function and Agency Size

**Category:** Benchmarks  
**Research ID:** `research-05`  
**Tier:** 1  
**Updated:** 2026-04-11

## Overview

2024-2025 time waste estimates for key agency operational functions across small, mid, and large agency sizes

## Related Notes

[[research-01]] · [[research-06]] · [[research-03]]

## Key Facts

### 1. Small agencies spend 10-20 hours per week on client reporting, mid-size agencies 20-40 hours, and large agencies 40+ hours

**Context:** AgencyAnalytics data from 7,000 surveyed agencies shows automation can save 137 hours per month (~30 hours per week)
**Use Case:** Reporting automation ROI by agency size
**Source:** AgencyAnalytics 7,000-agency benchmark + Teamwork productivity reports
**hours per week small:** 10-20
**hours per week mid:** 20-40
**hours per week large:** 40+
**automation savings:** 137 hours/month (~30 hrs/week)

### 2. Lead qualification consumes 5-10 hours/week at small agencies, 10-15 hours at mid-size, and 15-25 hours at large agencies

**Context:** Manual BANT/GPCT reviews and fortnightly alignment sessions drive most of the cost
**Use Case:** Lead qualification automation ROI
**Source:** Lead qualification process benchmarks
**hours per week small:** 5-10
**hours per week mid:** 10-15
**hours per week large:** 15-25

### 3. Client onboarding costs 5-10 hours per new client manually, scaling to 25-50 hours/month at mid-size agencies onboarding 10+ clients

**Context:** Onboarding time scales linearly with client volume and remains one of the highest per-client time drains
**Use Case:** Onboarding automation quick-win pitch
**Source:** OnboardMap service business onboarding studies
**hours per client:** 5-10
**hours per month mid:** 25-50 (10 clients)
**hours per month large:** 50-100

### 4. Invoicing and billing manual work consumes 5-10 hours/week at small agencies, 10-20 at mid-size, and 20-40 at large agencies

**Context:** 48% of agencies report billable tracking as a significant operational pain point
**Use Case:** Billing automation ROI and pain quantification
**Source:** AgencyAnalytics benchmark + industry billing surveys
**hours per week small:** 5-10
**hours per week mid:** 10-20
**hours per week large:** 20-40

### 5. Project management updates consume 8-15 hours/week at small agencies, 15-30 at mid-size, and 30-50 at large agencies

**Context:** Manual status checks and real-time updates typically take 1-3 hours per report
**Use Case:** PM automation ROI for operations
**Source:** PM productivity studies + agency owner surveys
**hours per week small:** 8-15
**hours per week mid:** 15-30
**hours per week large:** 30-50
**time per report:** 1-3 hours

### 6. Agency leaders spend 69% of their time in meetings and team management

**Context:** Internal communication overhead is the single largest drain on leadership bandwidth
**Use Case:** Leadership capacity unlock through automated updates and reports
**Source:** Agency leadership time-tracking studies
**percent leader time:** 69%
**hours per week small:** 10-20
**hours per week mid:** 20-30
**hours per week large:** 30-50

### 7. Data entry between tools costs small agencies 10-20 hours/week, mid-size 20-40, and large 40+ hours

**Context:** Root cause of most reporting waste — agencies typically juggle 5+ tools with no automation bridge
**Use Case:** Tool integration automation opportunity
**Source:** AgencyAnalytics benchmark
**tools per agency:** 5+
**hours per week small:** 10-20
**hours per week mid:** 20-40
**hours per week large:** 40+

### 8. Proposal generation consumes 5-15 hours/week at small agencies, 15-25 at mid-size, and 25-40 at large agencies

**Context:** Manual per-client proposal creation with limited templating
**Use Case:** Proposal automation ROI
**Source:** Industry proposal workflow estimates
**hours per week small:** 5-15
**hours per week mid:** 15-25
**hours per week large:** 25-40

### 9. Content repurposing takes 5-10 hours/week at small agencies, 10-20 at mid-size, and 20-30 at large agencies

**Context:** Manual transformation of content across channels — one of the highest AI-automation upside areas
**Use Case:** Content automation pitch for marketing and creative agencies
**Source:** Creative agency time-tracking reports
**hours per week small:** 5-10
**hours per week mid:** 10-20
**hours per week large:** 20-30

### 10. Status meetings consume 10-15 hours/week at small agencies, 15-25 at mid-size, and 25+ at large agencies

**Context:** Part of the broader 69% leader time spent in meetings and team management
**Use Case:** Meeting reduction through automated status reporting
**Source:** Leadership productivity benchmarks
**hours per week small:** 10-15
**hours per week mid:** 15-25
**hours per week large:** 25+

### 11. Small agencies waste proportionally more time on manual operations — typically 20-30 hours per week total due to limited automation infrastructure

**Context:** Small agencies often have the most to gain from automation because they lack the budgets for enterprise tools
**Use Case:** Small agency urgency framing and opportunity sizing
**Source:** Agency productivity research
**total manual hours small:** 20-30

### 12. Mid and large agencies scale manual operations linearly with client and staff count, often hitting 100+ hours/week of cumulative manual work

**Context:** Scale amplifies manual operations cost — each new client adds compounding manual overhead
**Use Case:** Scaling wall identification for growing agencies
**Source:** Aggregated benchmark reports
**total manual hours mid:** 50-100+
**total manual hours large:** 100+

### 13. Agencies typically struggle to achieve 50% billable utilization due to non-billable manual operations consuming half of staff time

**Context:** The inverse side of manual work: every manual hour is a billable hour lost
**Use Case:** Revenue drag framing in audits
**Source:** Teamwork productivity research
**billable utilization ceiling:** 50%
