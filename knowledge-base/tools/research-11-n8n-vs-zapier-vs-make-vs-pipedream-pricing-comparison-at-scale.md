## Key Facts

### 1. n8n Cloud offers 2,500 free executions per month, with paid tiers starting at €24/month for 10,000 executions and scaling to €650+ for 100,000+ executions

**Context:** n8n Cloud pricing is execution-based, not task-based — one execution can contain many steps
**Use Case:** n8n Cloud cost quoting for prospects
**Source:** n8n pricing page 2024-2025
**free tier:** 2,500 executions/month
**starter:** €24/month (10k executions)
**enterprise:** €650+/month (100k+)

### 2. Zapier's free tier allows only 100 tasks per month, with paid tiers ranging from $20 (750 tasks) to $800+ (250k tasks) plus $3,000+/month for overages

**Context:** Zapier counts each action step as a separate task, meaning multi-step workflows consume quotas 5-8x faster than expected
**Use Case:** Zapier cost comparison in sales objection handling
**Source:** Zapier pricing 2024-2025
**free tier:** 100 tasks/month
**starter:** $20/month (750 tasks)
**enterprise:** $800+/month
**overage ceiling:** $3,000+/month

### 3. Make.com offers 1,000 free operations per month, scaling from $10/month (10k ops) to $2,700/month for 800,000 operations

**Context:** Make charges per 'operation' — similar to Zapier's task model but often cheaper at mid-scale
**Use Case:** Make comparison for mid-scale agencies
**Source:** Make.com pricing 2024-2025
**free tier:** 1,000 operations/month
**starter:** $10/month (10k ops)
**enterprise ceiling:** $2,700/month (800k ops)

### 4. Pipedream's free tier gives 100 credits/month, with starter at $29/month (2k credits) and pro from $49/month with unlimited workflows

**Context:** Pipedream uses a credit-per-execution model that's developer-friendly but less intuitive for no-code users
**Use Case:** Developer-focused platform comparison
**Source:** Pipedream pricing 2024-2025
**free tier:** 100 credits/month
**starter:** $29/month (2k credits)
**pro:** $49+/month unlimited workflows

### 5. Self-hosted n8n has zero platform license fees — only server infrastructure costs of €50-200/month depending on scale

**Context:** The single biggest cost advantage in the automation space
**Use Case:** Self-hosting value pitch for scaling agencies
**Source:** n8n self-hosting documentation + hosting benchmarks
**license cost:** €0
**server cost monthly:** €50-200

### 6. At 10,000 tasks/month, Make.com is cheapest at $11/month, followed by n8n Cloud at $26, Pipedream at $29, and Zapier at $49

**Context:** At low volume, all platforms are roughly comparable — differentiation comes at scale
**Use Case:** Low-volume cost comparison
**Source:** 2024-2025 platform pricing comparisons
**make:** $11/month
**n8n cloud:** $26/month
**pipedream:** $29/month
**zapier:** $49/month
**self hosted fixed:** $100/month (break-even threshold not yet crossed)

### 7. At 50,000 tasks/month, n8n self-hosted costs ~$100 fixed while Zapier costs $200-400, Make costs $50-100, Pipedream $49+, and n8n Cloud $60

**Context:** Mid-scale is where self-hosting starts winning — the fixed cost model flips the economics
**Use Case:** Mid-scale cost flip demonstration
**Source:** Platform pricing analyses 2024-2025
**n8n self hosted:** $100 fixed
**zapier:** $200-400
**make:** $50-100
**n8n cloud:** $60

### 8. At 200,000 tasks/month, self-hosted n8n costs $150-200 while Zapier hits $1,000+, Make exceeds $500, and n8n Cloud ~$300

**Context:** High volume: self-hosted n8n is 5-10x cheaper than Zapier
**Use Case:** High-volume cost savings pitch
**Source:** Platform pricing comparisons

### 9. At 1 million tasks/month, self-hosted n8n costs $200-400 while Zapier costs $3,000+, Make costs $2,700, and n8n Cloud costs $650+

**Context:** Enterprise scale: self-hosted n8n saves agencies $30,000+/year versus Zapier

**make:** $2,700

### 10. Self-hosted n8n breaks even versus Make.com at approximately 20,000 tasks/month and versus Zapier at ~15,000 tasks/month

**Context:** These are the exact thresholds where OpsRadar's self-hosted model becomes mathematically superior
**Use Case:** Break-even math for migration pitches
**Source:** 2025 automation platform cost analyses
**breakeven vs make:** 20k tasks/month

### 11. Self-hosted n8n saves agencies approximately 75% versus Zapier at 100,000 tasks/month

**Context:** The cost savings at scale are dramatic enough to fund an entire automation service
**Use Case:** ROI pitch for retainer clients

**Context:** This multiplier effect is the primary reason Zapier becomes uneconomic at volume
**Use Case:** Hidden cost disclosure in sales conversations
**Source:** Zapier billing documentation + user reports
**task multiplier:** 5-8x per workflow run
