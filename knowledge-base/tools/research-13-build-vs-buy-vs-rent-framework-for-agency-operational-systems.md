## Key Facts

### 1. The build vs buy vs rent decision framework scores each option 1-5 across: strategic edge, change frequency, integration risk, and differentiation value

**Context:** High strategic edge plus frequent changes plus high integration risk equals build; stable and low-differentiation equals rent SaaS; mid-tier or mixed scenarios favor done-for-you services
**Use Case:** Decision framework for prospect conversations
**Source:** LinkedIn exec decision models + AI implementation primers 2024-2025
**framework factors:** Strategic edge, Change frequency, Integration risk, Differentiation value
**scoring:** 1-5 per factor

### 2. Agencies should BUILD in-house only for proprietary workflows that drive competitive differentiation (e.g., unique client scoring systems) where control directly improves revenue per employee

**Context:** Build is justified only when the automation is the agency's unfair advantage
**Use Case:** Distinguishing core vs commodity automation
**Source:** Agency strategy frameworks
**when to build:** Proprietary workflows with direct competitive edge

### 3. Agencies should BUY SaaS for commodity operations like reporting dashboards and payment processing — quick 90-day value but scales to $3,000+/month at 1M tasks with step multipliers

**Context:** SaaS wins when the workflow is a commodity and scale is predictable
**Use Case:** When to recommend SaaS over done-for-you
**Source:** SaaS implementation benchmarks
**when to buy:** Commodity workflows like reporting, payments
**time to value:** 90 days
**cost ceiling:** $3,000+/month at scale

### 4. Agencies should RENT done-for-you services when facing non-core operations overload, burnout, and need fastest ROI — typically achieving 3-month payback

**Context:** Done-for-you wins on speed and focus preservation
**Use Case:** Positioning OpsRadar as the done-for-you winner
**Source:** Done-for-you automation case studies
**when to rent:** Non-core operations causing overload or burnout
**payback timeline:** 3 months

### 5. Building automation in-house costs $100,000-$200,000 in year one, ballooning to $400,000+ over 3 years and $800,000+ over 5 years due to maintenance, silos, and training overhead

**Context:** The TCO of build grows exponentially because 95% of effort happens post-launch
**Use Case:** 5-year TCO argument against building
**Source:** Gartner-aligned TCO models + agency post-mortems
**year 1:** $100k-200k
**year 3 total:** $400k+
**year 5 total:** $800k+
**maintenance share:** 50%
**silos training share:** 20%

### 6. Buying SaaS costs $20,000-$50,000 in year one, scaling to $150,000 over 3 years and $300,000 over 5 years — primarily driven by usage overages and multi-step task inflation

**Context:** SaaS is cheap to start but grows linearly with usage
**Use Case:** Long-term SaaS cost warning
**Source:** SaaS pricing analyses 2024-2025
**year 1:** $20k-50k
**year 3 total:** $150k
**year 5 total:** $300k
**primary cost driver:** Usage overages and multi-step inflation

### 7. Renting done-for-you services costs $30,000-$60,000 in year one, $200,000 over 3 years, and $350,000 over 5 years — with 20-30% efficiency gains offsetting the fees

**Context:** Done-for-you has the lowest 3-year TCO when efficiency gains are factored in
**Use Case:** DFY as the economic winner over 3 years
**Source:** Done-for-you service provider case studies
**year 1:** $30k-60k
**year 3 total:** $200k
**year 5 total:** $350k
**efficiency gain:** 20-30%

**Use Case:** Hidden cost disclosure for DIY objection handling

### 11. Hybrid winning approach: Build the core differentiation (unique IP), rent the commodity layers (infrastructure, auth, integrations) — this pattern achieves top RPE through 60-70% billable utilization

**Context:** The smartest agencies don't pick one strategy — they blend all three
**Use Case:** Positioning OpsRadar as part of the hybrid winning strategy
**Source:** Top agency case studies 2024-2025
**optimal pattern:** Build core + Rent commodity + Buy SaaS for standard tools
**billable utilization target:** 60-70%

### 12. Decision matrix summary: BUILD when it's your core competitive edge, BUY SaaS when it's pure commodity, RENT done-for-you when you need speed + ops focus + maintenance included

**Context:** Three-line decision framework agency owners can apply instantly
**Use Case:** Simple framework for founder conversations
**Source:** Synthesized from 2024-2025 decision frameworks
**build rule:** Core competitive edge only
**buy rule:** Pure commodity workflows
**rent rule:** Speed + ops focus + maintenance
